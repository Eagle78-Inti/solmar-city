An original open-world crime game built for the make.fun **Make GTA 6** bounty.
You are Rook, a getaway driver working for the fixer Mari Voss in Solmar, a
sun-soaked bay city of towers, neon streets, beaches and piers.

## Play it

Serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html` in desktop
Chrome. No network is needed: three.js is vendored under `game/vendor/`,
every texture is generated in code, and every sound is synthesized live.

## Controls

- **WASD** move / drive, **SHIFT** sprint, **SPACE** handbrake
- **E** steal or exit a car, **T** start a taxi job, **CLICK** shoot
- **R** reset the car, **ESC** pause, **M** mute

## What is in the city

- Drive and walk a bay city: towers, neon streets, beach, three piers
- Traffic and pedestrians who flee trouble; cars to steal
- Wanted stars, police chases, a bust timer and a helicopter at 4 stars
- Five story missions for the fixer Mari Voss, with cash, respect and a double-cross
- Taxi jobs, street races, 20 hidden Solmar coins, combat, a day/night cycle
- HUD with cash, health, speed, objective, clock and a live minimap; save
